# Privacy Policy — ALSA Match Results

ALSA Match Results is an independent, open-source desktop application for preparing Discord match result messages.

## Information stored locally

Player and team names, Discord IDs and mentions, and message templates are stored locally on your device. The application does not transmit this information to the developer or any server.

## Clipboard

When you select “Copy message”, the generated text is placed on your device’s clipboard. The application does not send messages to Discord; you decide where to paste and share them.

## Accounts and tracking

The application requires no account, Discord login or bot token. It contains no advertising, analytics or telemetry.

## Deleting your data

You can remove saved entries in the application. To delete all saved settings and contacts, close the application and delete:

%APPDATA%\ResultadoDaLiga\preferencias.json

## Third-party services

Downloading the application through GitHub and sharing messages through Discord are governed by those services’ privacy policies.
